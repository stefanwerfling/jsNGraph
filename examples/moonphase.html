Whale and dolphin sightings by moon phase

Four years of (invented) whale-watching trips out of Valle Gran Rey, sorted by the age of the moon on the day of the trip. The moons circle the Earth at the top; below, the ring rises and turns red where a species was seen more often (or in bigger groups) than on average, and sinks into blue where it was seen less. Drag to turn, hover a phase.
