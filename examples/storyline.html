Project storyline

Series:

Ten years of a (made-up) open-source project: every contributor is a band that runs through the releases, docs pushes and meetups they took part in; the band width is their share of the work there. Hover a band or a chip to follow one person, click it to see only their events and everyone they worked with, click an event bar for its series, double-click the background to go back. Wheel zooms, drag pans. new StorylineChart(el, {data}) — canvas, no dependencies.
