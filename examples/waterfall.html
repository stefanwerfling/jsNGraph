Waterfall spectra

Fifty spectra (an FFT of a drifting test signal each) stacked front to back. Hover a trace to see it below left, click to keep it; drag the marker to cut a slice through every spectrum at one frequency (below right). Wheel zooms the frequency axis, drag pans, double-click resets. new WaterfallChart(el, {series}) + LineChart — canvas, no dependencies.

Data is generated in the page: a sum of three sines whose periods drift from trace to trace, plus noise, through a radix-2 FFT, in dB, floored at −30.
