Editable board

new RectBoard(el, {editable: true, grid: 20}): drag cards (Alt: no grid), resize at the corner, drag from a side dot to connect, select a connector and drag its end to reroute. Shift-click toggles, Ctrl/⌘-drag selects, Shift-drag on empty space draws a frame. Moving the frame carries what is inside (dragPartners). Every gesture fires commit; RectHistory gives Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y.
