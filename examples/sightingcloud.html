Sighting cloud — ten seasons as one volume

Every sighting of ten invented years around an invented island becomes a puff of light in a box: east and north on the floor, the day of the year going up. Where a species keeps coming back to the same place at the same time, the puffs add up into a glowing body — resident pilot whales are a column standing in the canyon, Bryde's whales a summer cloud drifting east, common dolphins a winter band along the north coast. The picture is ray-marched on the GPU from a 3D texture: cut the box, look through it, or turn it into solid surfaces.

Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, double-click to reset; hover the cloud for the densest point under the pointer. Island, canyon, species patterns and all sightings are invented.
