A morning whale-watching trip off La Gomera

The boat leaves the harbour of Vueltas in Valle Gran Rey, follows its route along the west coast and comes back three hours later. Its radar (bottom left, north up, the coast in yellow) picks up blows, splashes and the shearwaters that give a feeding school away; every group the boat comes close to goes into the sightings log.
