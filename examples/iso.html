Isometric architecture — a made-up web shop

Devices and services stand on a tile grid as 3D icons, regions mark zones on the floor, labels float above their items. Every icon is drawn by the library from a few isometric solids and can take any colour — no image files. This is milestone I1 of the isometric editor (viewing); connectors and editing come next.

Drag to pan, wheel to zoom, double-click the floor to fit, click an item to select it (Shift adds), double-click an item to fly to it. Hover an icon on the right to light the items using it.
