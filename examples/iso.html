Isometric architecture — a made-up web shop

Devices and services stand on a tile grid as 3D icons, regions mark zones on the floor, labels float above their items. Every icon is drawn by the library from a few isometric solids and can take any colour — no image files. Connectors are routed along the grid around the other items, with arrows lying on the floor, labels and travelling dots. It is an editor: drag icons in from the kit, move things around (connectors re-route live), draw connectors, zones and floor text, edit labels in place, undo — or switch to View for a plain viewer.

Drag an item to move it (snaps to tiles, never onto another item), drag the floor to pan, Shift-drag for a lasso, wheel to zoom, double-click to edit a label. A selected connector shows handles: drag the small dot on a segment to add a waypoint, drag a waypoint to move it (double-click removes it), drag an end to re-attach. A selected zone has corner handles.
