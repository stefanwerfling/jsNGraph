The Unix family tree

Fifty-odd years of operating systems on one time axis: every system appears as a dot in the year of its first release, a successor continues its parent's line, offshoots branch off, and a line ends where a system was discontinued. Hover a system to light its ancestry and descendants. Years are approximate first releases and the tree is simplified to one parent per system.
