Radar charts

One axis per category around a centre, every series a closed profile — also known as spider, web or Kiviat chart. Hover a vertex for its value, click a legend entry to hide a series, wheel zooms, drag pans, double-click resets. new RadarChart(el, {categories, series}) — canvas, no dependencies.
