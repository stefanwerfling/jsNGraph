A year of contributions as a skyline

One column per day, laid out like a contribution calendar — weeks from left to right, weekdays from back to front — and raised by the day's count. Drag across the days to look at a range: the totals, the languages and the kinds of work below follow it. Hover a language to see the days it led. The developer and every number are made up.
