Orchard Row Juice Works — a made-up bottling plant, running

Fruit comes in by truck, is stored, washed, milled and pressed; the juice is pasteurised, buffered in tanks, filled, capped and labelled, packed into cases, palletised, wrapped and shipped. Forklifts, conveyors and pipes move every lot along the red flow lines; machines fail now and then and the technician walks over from the control room. Around the hall: line OEE, throughput, the order book and the automation pyramid from field level up to ERP — hover a level to see its devices in the hall.
