Veldmark city operations table

A made-up city on one glowing table: power use per district over the day, traffic on the main roads, incidents and response times, wind and solar. Drag to orbit, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom, click a panel to fly to it, double-click for the whole table. The hour drives every panel.
