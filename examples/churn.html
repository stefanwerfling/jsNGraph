Predicting churn

Every strand is one description of a customer — a single attribute (“contract = one year”) or two together. Its length is how many customers match, its colour how they ended: red from the centre outwards up to the share that left, cyan for the rest, and the dot in the colour of the majority. Strands where most customers left fill the top half, the others the bottom half — strongest at the right, so reading counter-clockwise from the right the churn probability falls all the way round. Narrow the sliders to keep only frequent, clear or strongly lifted descriptions.
