Encounter rose — where did they go after we met them?

Every tracked encounter of one invented season starts in the centre, at the moment of first contact. All of them are replayed on one shared clock: dolphins shoot outwards, pilot whales hardly move, sperm whales vanish on long dives (dashed), bow-riders circle back. The ring outside counts where everybody is right now, stacked by species; the arrows in the middle point where each species goes on average (longer = more agreement). Turn the dial to offshore or first heading to ask different questions with the same tracks.

Hover a track for its numbers, click to pin it, hover a sector of the outer ring to light its encounters, wheel to zoom the distance, double-click to reset. Species models, tracks and every number are invented.
