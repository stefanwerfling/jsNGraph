Social sky — who swims with whom

Every individual recognised by its dorsal fin is a star; the more often it was resighted, the brighter it burns. Animals seen together again and again are joined into constellations (half-weight index, the usual association measure for photo-ID data) and tight groups become named social units. Drag the time: stars rise as animals are first photographed, calves appear next to their mothers (gold dashes), the latest encounters ring, animals not seen for two seasons fade.

Hover a star for its companions, a dotted outline for its unit; click a star for its card; wheel zooms (names appear), drag pans, double-click resets. Individuals, fins, units and every encounter are invented.
