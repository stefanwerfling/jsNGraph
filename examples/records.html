Bike supply chain

A (made-up) bicycle supply chain: every card is a site with the items it makes and their stock. Links run from an item to the items made from it — ore to steel to tubes to frames to bikes, and worn parts back to the smelter through the recycling yard.

Click an item or a link to light its whole flow, up- and downstream. Click it again (or the background) to clear.

Hover an item and press + to make one more: its stock rises, every input drops by one. When an input reaches 0 it turns red and production stops — keep the chain running.

The − in a frame's title bar folds the group into one box. Wheel zooms, drag pans, double-click fits.

new RecordGraph(el, {data, produce: true}) — canvas, no dependencies.
